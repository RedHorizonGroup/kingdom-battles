FIELD MANUAL

How to rule

  1. Crowns accumulate over time. There is no mining — income flows from the building families you own, minus the upkeep of the troops you store.
  2. Build to unlock. Each building family has a cost and a prerequisite array. Own every family a doctrine requires and that doctrine unlocks in the order tree.
  3. Equip your line. Weapon upgrades (long sword, lance, jeweled staff…) raise the attack, defense, and health of every troop you deploy.
  4. Earn your banners. Achievements unlock new classes only when their stated condition is met — some you will only reach deep in the tree.
  5. March. Between fights you grow the capital on the world map. Enter a level, deploy troops forward, and break the enemy keep before its pressure breaks yours.
  6. Seize a troop. Once a troop is on the field you may take direct control of it and press the attack yourself each turn.
  7. Lose and retry. Losing a level restarts that level. Each level you clear grants +1000 crowns toward the next.
